Progressive Overload Guide
📈
When to Add Weight
When you complete all sets at the top rep range (e.g. 4×12) with perfect form — add 2.5 kg next session.
⏱
Rest Periods
Compound lifts: 2–3 min rest. Isolation work: 60–90 sec. Keep rest consistent to measure progress.
📋
Track Every Session
Log weight, sets, reps. You can't manage what you don't measure. Review weekly to identify plateaus.
Coach's Corner — Evidence-Based Advice

Everything below is backed by sports science, not social media trends.

😴
Sleep is the Real Anabolic
80% of growth hormone is released during deep sleep. Aim for 7–9 hours. Consistent sleep and wake times matter more than duration alone. A single bad night drops testosterone by 10–15%.
🥩
Protein Distribution
Muscle protein synthesis peaks with 30–40g protein per meal. Spreading protein across 4 meals is more effective than two large hits. Have 20–40g within 2 hours post-workout.
🔥
Cardio Without Killing Gains
Keep cardio sessions to 20–30 min at 60–70% max HR (zone 2). This preserves muscle while burning fat. Avoid long runs on leg day — do it after upper body or on rest days.
🧠
Mind-Muscle Connection
Consciously squeezing the target muscle during each rep increases activation by up to 20%. Slow the eccentric (lowering) phase to 3 seconds — this is where most growth happens.
⚡
Pre-Workout Nutrition
Eat a balanced meal 2–3 hours before training. 45–60 min before: fast carbs + 20g protein. Caffeine 200–400mg peaks at 45–60 min — time it right or skip it if you train late.
🦵
Mobility is Performance
5 min dynamic warm-up before lifting + 5 min static stretch after. Poor hip mobility limits squat depth. Poor shoulder mobility limits press performance. Fix the root cause.
📉
Avoid Overtraining
Signs: elevated resting HR, poor sleep, strength drops, constant soreness, mood changes. Solution: deload week every 8–12 weeks — 50% volume at normal intensity. Growth happens during recovery.
🏆
The 80/20 Rule
80% of results come from: consistent training, adequate protein, good sleep, progressive overload. Supplements, timing tricks, and advanced techniques make up the other 20%. Master basics first.
🌡️
Creatine — Just Do It
Most researched supplement in sports science. 5g/day consistently (no loading needed). Increases strength output by 5–15%. Safe for kidneys in healthy people. Takes 3–4 weeks to fully saturate.
🔄
Periodisation
Rotate training phases every 8–12 weeks: Hypertrophy (8–12 reps), Strength (3–6 reps), Endurance (15–20 reps). This prevents adaptation and breaks plateaus systematically.
🫀
Stress Kills Gains
Cortisol is catabolic — it breaks down muscle. Chronic stress, under-eating, or overtraining all spike cortisol. Manage stress actively: cold exposure, breathing drills, adequate rest days.
🎯
Form Before Weight
Injuries set you back 4–12 weeks minimum. Ego lifting is the biggest progress killer in any gym. Record your sets. Watch them back. Fix form flaws before adding load.

Hydration Timing Protocol
Signs of Dehydration
⚠️
Early Signs (–1 to –2%)
Thirst, darker urine, slight fatigue, reduced concentration. Performance drops by 5–8%. Don't wait until you're thirsty — you're already behind.
🔴
Moderate Signs (–3 to –5%)
Headache, dizziness, muscle cramps, reduced strength. Anaerobic performance drops 10–20%. This is where most gym-goers unknowingly train.
💧
Urine Color Guide
Pale yellow = well hydrated. Dark yellow = drink now. Brown = urgent. Clear = slightly overhydrated (rare risk). Aim for pale lemonade color throughout the day.
